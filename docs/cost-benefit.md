# Cost-Benefit Analysis & ROI

**Project:** GWA Calculator & Academic Tracker

## 1. Development Costs (Year 0)
* **Labor Costs:** 5 Developers × 40 hours × ₱250/hr = ₱50,000
* **Software Licenses & Tools:** ₱0 (Utilized open-source Python, React Native, and free GitHub tier)
* **Total Development Cost:** **₱50,000**

## 2. Operational Costs (Year 1 Projected)
* **Cloud Hosting (PaaS):** ₱3,000 / year
* **Database Management:** ₱2,000 / year
* **Routine Maintenance & Bug Fixes:** ₱5,000 / year
* **Total Operational Cost:** **₱10,000 / year**

## 3. Tangible Benefits (Year 1 Projected)
* **Premium Subscription Revenue:** 150 student users opting for the premium "Ad-Free & Advanced Analytics" subscription tier at ₱500/year = ₱75,000
* **Total Tangible Benefits:** **₱75,000 / year**

## 4. Intangible Benefits
* **Reduced Student Anxiety:** Provides peace of mind through real-time academic standing visibility.
* **Better Academic Planning:** Empowers students to make data-driven decisions on which subjects to prioritize to reach their target GWA.
* **Skill Development:** Provided the development team with hands-on agile software management experience.

## 5. ROI Calculation (1-Year Projection)
* **Total Project Costs (Year 1):** Dev Cost (₱50,000) + Ops Cost (₱10,000) = ₱60,000
* **Total Project Benefits (Year 1):** ₱75,000
* **Net Benefit:** ₱75,000 - ₱60,000 = ₱15,000
* **ROI Formula:** (Net Benefit / Total Costs) × 100
* **Return on Investment (ROI):** (15,000 / 60,000) × 100 = **25%**

## 6. Final Recommendation
Based on the projected 1-year ROI of 25%, the project is financially viable. The transition to a premium subscription-based model successfully covers both operational and initial development costs within the first twelve months, while providing immense intangible value to the student body. 

**Recommendation: Proceed with full production deployment and marketing.**
