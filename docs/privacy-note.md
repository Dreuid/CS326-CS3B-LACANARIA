# Data Privacy Note

**Project:** GWA Calculator & Academic Tracker

## Commitment to User Privacy
Our team recognizes that academic grades are highly sensitive personal information. We are committed to adhering to standard data protection principles, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), to ensure our users' data remains secure.

## Data Collection & Usage
* **What we collect:** Account email addresses, course names, unit counts, and self-reported grades.
* **How we use it:** Strictly to calculate the General Weighted Average (GWA) and provide academic trajectory insights to the user.
* **What we DO NOT do:** We do not sell user data to third-party advertisers, nor do we share individual academic records with university administration without the user utilizing the explicit "Export PDF" feature.

## Security Measures
All academic data stored in our SQL database is associated with a securely hashed user ID. With our transition to a premium subscription-based model, we have eliminated the need for third-party ad trackers, further insulating our users' data from external surveillance.
